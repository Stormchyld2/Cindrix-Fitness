Bar Squat

Squat pattern · Heavy band · 3 × 8–10

Sample illustration. Final photos and clips replace it.
1Band under both feet and over the bar at your collarbones.
2Elbows forward. Sit between the hips with knees over toes.
3Drive up for 1 s, hold 1 s, then return slowly over 3 s.
