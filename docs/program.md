# Tension Band Workout Program (12 Weeks)

Source of truth for the Cindrix Fitness app content. Written 2026-11-20.

## Overview

This program builds full-body strength in 12 weeks using the gear you already own. You train three days a week, 35-45 minutes a session, alternating Workout A and Workout B.

Every session covers the same six jobs: squat, hinge, push, pull, core and shoulder health. Progress comes from three levers, in this order: more reps, slower tempo, then a heavier band. Every fourth week is an easy week so joints and tendons catch up.

**Gear used**

- Loop bands in light, medium and heavy (stack two for more load)
- 5-ring door anchor
- Handles
- Weighted bar
- Leg straps (ankle attachments)

**Assumptions:** healthy, has trained before, wants general strength and durability. Ring 1 is the lowest ring on the anchor and ring 5 the highest. If the gear differs, change the ring numbers; the movements stay the same.

## Setup

Set the door anchor so every pull pushes the door into its frame, then match each exercise to a ring and a band.

**Door anchor safety**

1. Use a solid door that closes and latches. Lock it if you can.
2. Stand on the side the door swings away from. The anchor cap sits on the far side, so a pull presses the door into its stop instead of opening it.
3. Before the first set, load the anchor with a slow body-weight pull and watch the door and cap.
4. Keep the strap flat and untwisted, and check that the door still latches fully with the anchor in place.

**Ring map**

| Ring | Height | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Ankle | Glute kickback, hip abduction (leg straps) |
| 2 | Hip | Spare: pull-throughs for extra glute work |
| 3 | Chest | Chest press, row, Pallof press |
| 4 | Face / above | Face pull |
| 5 | Overhead | Lat pulldown, chop |

**Starting band by exercise**

| Exercise | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| Bar squat, bar Romanian deadlift | Heavy (or two mediums) |
| Standing row | Heavy |
| Chest press, lat pulldown, overhead press | Medium |
| Split squat | Medium under the front foot |
| Pallof press, chop | Light to medium |
| Glute kickback, hip abduction, face pull | Light |

**Picking the right band:** the band should be tight at the start, never slack. The last two reps of the target range should be hard while form holds. If you finish the top of the range with reps to spare, go up a band or stack another.

**Bar and straps:** for bar lifts, loop the band under both feet and over the bar. Keep the bar close to the body and the band centered under the feet. Fit the leg strap just above the ankle bone, snug but not tight.

## Warm-up and cooldown

Spend 6 minutes warming up before every session and 4 minutes cooling down after it.

**Warm-up (6 minutes)**

1. March in place or step side to side, 1 minute
2. Band pull-aparts with a light band, 2 sets of 15
3. Cat-cow, 8 slow reps
4. Body-weight squat with a 2-second pause at the bottom, 10 reps
5. Body-weight hip hinge, 10 reps
6. Shoulder dislocates with a light band held wide, 10 reps
7. One ramp-up set of the first exercise with a lighter band, 8 reps

**Cooldown (4 minutes)**

- Doorway chest stretch, 30 seconds each side
- Lat stretch holding the door frame, 30 seconds each side
- Standing hamstring stretch, 30 seconds each leg
- Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch, 30 seconds each side
- Slow nose breathing, 1 minute

## Workouts A and B

Each workout is three paired exercises (supersets). Do one exercise, then its partner, rest 60 seconds, and repeat for all sets before moving to the next pair. Sets, reps and tempo come from the weekly table below.

**Tempo** is written drive-hold-return in seconds. 1-1-3 means 1 second to drive, 1 second hold at peak tension, 3 seconds controlled return.

### Workout A: squat and push focus

| Pair | Exercise | Setup | Cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1A | Bar squat | Band under both feet and over the bar, bar at collarbones | Elbows forward, sit between the hips, knees over toes, drive up |
| 1B | Kneeling lat pulldown | Ring 5, face the door, handles overhead | Pull elbows to ribs, squeeze 1 second |
| 2A | Standing chest press, split stance | Ring 3, back to the door, one foot forward | Brace the core, press straight out, switch lead foot each set |
| 2B | Glute kickback | Ring 1, leg strap, face the door, hold a support | Kick straight back, squeeze, keep the low back flat |
| 3A | Pallof press | Ring 3, stand side-on, handle at chest | Press out and resist turning, hold 2 seconds, both sides |
| 3B | Face pull with rotation | Ring 4, handles at eye level | Pull to ears, rotate fists up, squeeze 1 second |

### Workout B: hinge and pull focus

| Pair | Exercise | Setup | Cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1A | Bar Romanian deadlift | Band under both feet and over the bar, bar at thighs | Hips back, bar slides down the thighs, flat back, drive hips forward |
| 1B | Bar overhead press | Stand on the band, bar at collarbones | Ribs down, glutes tight, press straight overhead |
| 2A | Standing row | Ring 3, feet staggered, handles in hand | Pull to the ribs, squeeze shoulder blades together |
| 2B | Split squat | Band under the front foot and over the bar, back foot behind | Torso upright, back knee toward the floor, switch legs each set |
| 3A | High-to-low chop | Ring 5, stand side-on, hands together | Rotate through the hips, arms mostly straight, pivot the back foot |
| 3B | Standing hip abduction | Ring 1, leg strap on the outside ankle | Lift out to the side, no leaning, 3 seconds back down |

**Optional arms finisher** (either day, 2 sets of 12-15): biceps curl standing on a band, then triceps pressdown at ring 5.

**Unilateral work:** for single-leg or single-side moves, the reps and sets listed are per side.

## 12-week progression

Three 4-week blocks, each ending in an easy week. Train Monday, Wednesday, Friday, alternating A and B, so week 1 is A-B-A and week 2 is B-A-B.

| Week | Phase | Sets | Reps | Tempo | Rest | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Foundation | 2 | 10-12 | 1-0-3 | 60 s | Learn setups, stop 3 reps short of failure |
| 2 | Foundation | 3 | 10-12 | 1-0-3 | 60 s | Add the third set |
| 3 | Foundation | 3 | 12-15 | 1-0-3 | 60 s | Go up a band if you hit 15 on every set |
| 4 | Easy week | 2 | 10 | 1-0-2 | 60 s | Lighter band, stop 4 reps short |
| 5 | Build | 3 | 8-10 | 1-1-3 | 75 s | Heavier band, 1 s hold at peak tension |
| 6 | Build | 3 | 10-12 | 1-1-3 | 75 s | Same band as week 5 |
| 7 | Build | 4 | 10-12 | 1-1-3 | 75 s | 4 sets on pairs 1 and 2, 3 on pair 3 |
| 8 | Easy week | 2 | 10 | 1-0-2 | 60 s | Drop back a band level |
| 9 | Peak | 4 | 8-10 | 1-1-3 | 90 s | Stack bands or go heavier |
| 10 | Peak | 4 | 10-12 | 1-1-3 | 90 s | Same load as week 9 |
| 11 | Peak | 4 | 12-15 | 1-1-3 | 90 s | Last set of each exercise 1 rep short of failure |
| 12 | Test | 3 | Test | 1-0-2 | 90 s | Retest the four baselines, then stop |

**Double progression rule:** when you hit the top of the rep range on every set with clean tempo, go up one band level next session. Drop back to the bottom of the range with the new band.

**Pain and form rules (no one to ask):**

- Muscle burn and shaking are normal. Sharp, pinching or joint pain means stop that set.
- Use a lighter band, shorten the range of motion, or swap in a similar movement that doesn't hurt.
- If pain returns the next session, skip that movement for the week and retry next week with a light band.
- If form breaks before the target reps, the band is too heavy. Never force reps with a sloppy setup.
- Missed a week? Repeat the last week you completed instead of jumping ahead.

## Band care and long-term use

Bands are consumables, so inspect them every session and keep spares. A snapped band under tension can cut skin or hit your face.

**Inspect before every session (30 seconds)**

- Stretch each band along its length and look for nicks, thin spots, cracks or a sticky surface
- Check handle clips, carabiners, anchor strap stitching and ring welds
- Retire any band with a nick or crack. Never tape or knot a damaged band

**Care**

- Store bands loose, away from sunlight, heat and sharp edges
- Wipe off sweat and dirt, and let them dry before storing
- Rotate bands so one heavy band doesn't take all the wear
- Don't stretch a band past about 2.5 times its resting length

**Spares for long-term use:** keep at least one spare of each band level in use, plus a spare handle and anchor strap. Assume you can't buy replacements.

**Improvised anchors with no door:**

- A tree, fence post or beam, wrapped with a wide strap or padded loop
- A wall stud or post ring bolted to framing
- Your own body weight: stand on the band with both feet for squat, deadlift and press movements
- Test every anchor with a slow pull before loading it, and keep your face out of the band's line
